simee · identity study

A cut emerald, two pyramids tall

The mark is a bipyramid: a short crown on a longer pavilion, meeting at a ten-sided girdle, with a second, twisted ring of facets on each pyramid. It stands one and a half times taller than it is wide. In the app it is the activity indicator, and every state below is motion or colour. It has no face and never gets one.

Crown, girdle, pavilion and culet are the cutter's names for the top, the widest ring, the bottom and the tip. The workbench uses the same terms.

01 · Shape study

Twelve cuts, from six facets to sixty

Every shape is two pyramids joined at a girdle. They differ in side count, crown and pavilion height, a girdle band, extra facet rings, and twist. Hover to turn one; click to open it in the workbench.

02 · Motion states

What the stone does while simee works

Each state maps to something simee is doing. Speed, colour, position and roll carry the meaning; the shape never changes. With reduced motion turned on, these start paused.
